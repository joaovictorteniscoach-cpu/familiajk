# EP01 — "Quem está dentro do ovo?"

**Duração:** 30s · **Formato:** 9:16 · **Blocos:** 3 × 10s
**Custo estimado:** 3 blocos × 20 = 60 créditos + narração 0,45 + música

> Em toda geração, `{ESTILO}` = a fórmula de estilo e `{PALETA}` = a trava de
> paleta, ambas em [`../02-biblia-visual.md`](../02-biblia-visual.md), coladas
> literalmente. Referências na ordem **clareira → Zuzu → Bibi → ovo**.

---

## Narração (uma linha por bloco, 19–21 palavras cada)

Voz: contadora de história calorosa, empolgada, ritmo rápido e sorridente.
Marcação de entrega: `warm thrilled storyteller, quick bouncy pace, bright
smiling timbre, bursting with delight`.

| # | Texto |
|---|---|
| 1 | Olha só, tem alguém dentro deste ovo gigante. [gasp] Escuta: toc, toc, toc. Quem será que está aí? Já apareceram duas orelhas! |
| 2 | As orelhas são compridas e macias. O ovo balança de um lado pro outro. Você já sabe quem é? Conta comigo! |
| 3 | É um coelhinho! [laughs] Ele pula, pisca e manda um beijo pra você. E olha, chegou outro ovo. Quem será agora? |

A pergunta fica no **fim** da linha 2 — a virada de bloco é a pausa da resposta,
e o bloco 3 abre com o pagamento. Nunca deixar pausa longa dentro da linha.

## Bloco 1 — 0 a 10s · O GANCHO

```
{ESTILO}
{PALETA}

SHOT 1 — 0.0s to 2.5s — WIDE, slight low angle: the sunny clearing; the huge
striped egg sits in the centre and ROCKS side to side on the grass from the
very first frame; Zuzu hops into frame from the left and freezes, both ears
shooting straight up in surprise.
HARD CUT.
SHOT 2 — 2.5s to 5.0s — CLOSE-UP on Zuzu: he turns to camera, eyes going wide,
one ear pointing back at the egg, then presses a tiny foot to his mouth in a
"shh" gesture.
HARD CUT.
SHOT 3 — 5.0s to 7.5s — EXTREME CLOSE-UP on the top of the egg: the zig-zag
crack opens a little wider with each knock, tiny shell flakes popping off.
HARD CUT.
SHOT 4 — 7.5s to 10.0s — MEDIUM: two long soft ears push out through the crack
and flop over the shell; Zuzu bounces on the spot, ears flapping.

MOTION: springy bouncy friendly motion (hops, wobbles, blinks), smooth gentle
camera moves; movement from the very first frame, no static opening.
AUDIO: soft knocking "toc toc" tied to the crack widening, a light sparkle ding
as the ears appear, gentle outdoor ambience — no voice, no narration, no music.
Characters only emote and gesture, they do NOT talk. No on-screen text.
```

**Legenda queimada (bloco 1):** `TEM ALGUÉM NO OVO!` aparecendo em 0,3s.

## Bloco 2 — 10 a 20s · A PISTA E A PARTICIPAÇÃO

```
{ESTILO}
{PALETA}

SHOT 1 — 0.0s to 2.5s — MEDIUM: the egg rocks harder, tipping left and right;
Bibi flies in, circles it once and lands on top holding on with her legs.
HARD CUT.
SHOT 2 — 2.5s to 5.0s — EXTREME CLOSE-UP on one ear: it twitches, brushes a
flower and springs back, showing how soft it is.
HARD CUT.
SHOT 3 — 5.0s to 7.5s — WIDE, high angle looking down: the whole clearing, the
egg spinning slowly on the grass, Zuzu circling it while Bibi hangs on.
HARD CUT.
SHOT 4 — 7.5s to 10.0s — CLOSE-UP on Zuzu and Bibi side by side: both turn and
look straight into the camera, Bibi raising both antennae and Zuzu nodding
fast, inviting the viewer to answer.

MOTION: springy bouncy friendly motion, gentle push-in on the final shot;
movement from the very first frame.
AUDIO: wobble and rocking sounds, a soft whoosh as Bibi flies, a rising three-
note chime under the final look-to-camera — no voice, no narration, no music.
Characters only emote and gesture, they do NOT talk. No on-screen text.
```

**Legenda queimada (bloco 2):** `CONTA COMIGO!` nos últimos 2s.

## Bloco 3 — 20 a 30s · A REVELAÇÃO E O LOOP

```
{ESTILO}
{PALETA}

SHOT 1 — 0.0s to 2.5s — CLOSE-UP: the shell splits and a small round glossy
white rabbit with huge sparkling eyes and pink inner ears pops up, blinking.
HARD CUT.
SHOT 2 — 2.5s to 5.0s — WIDE: the rabbit leaps out and bounces three times
across the clearing; Zuzu and Bibi jump and cheer, confetti of flower petals
puffing up around them.
HARD CUT.
SHOT 3 — 5.0s to 7.5s — MEDIUM, high angle: the rabbit stops, turns to camera,
winks and blows a kiss with one paw; a sparkle pops where the kiss lands.
HARD CUT.
SHOT 4 — 7.5s to 10.0s — EXTREME CLOSE-UP then pulling back to MEDIUM: a brand
new striped egg rolls into frame from the right and stops, already rocking;
Zuzu's ears shoot up again in the same pose as the very first shot.

MOTION: springy bouncy friendly motion, celebratory pops, camera pulls back on
the last shot; movement from the very first frame.
AUDIO: a bright shell-crack pop, rising marimba chord and tiny clapping on the
reveal, then the same sparkle ding from the opening as the new egg arrives —
no voice, no narration, no music.
Characters only emote and gesture, they do NOT talk. No on-screen text.
```

**Legenda queimada (bloco 3):** `UM COELHINHO!` na revelação, e
`QUEM SERÁ AGORA?` nos últimos 2s.

> O último plano repete a pose do primeiro. É de propósito: o vídeo volta
> visualmente ao começo, e é isso que faz o replay.
