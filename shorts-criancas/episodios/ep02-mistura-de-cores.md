# EP02 — "De que cor vai ficar?"

**Duração:** 30s · **Formato:** 9:16 · **Blocos:** 3 × 10s
**Objeto-condutor:** dois potes de tinta que viram um só.

> `{ESTILO}` e `{PALETA}` vêm de [`../02-biblia-visual.md`](../02-biblia-visual.md).
> Referências na ordem **clareira → Zuzu → Bibi → potes de tinta**.

## Narração

| # | Texto |
|---|---|
| 1 | Olha, o Zuzu achou duas tintas na clareira. Uma azul bem forte, outra amarela. Adivinha o que vai acontecer quando misturar! |
| 2 | A Bibi mexe com o palito e a tinta roda no potinho. Já está mudando de cor. Fala junto comigo: qual? |
| 3 | Verde! Azul com amarelo vira verde, igualzinho à grama. E olha, chegou um pote vermelho. Que cor vai sair agora? |

HARD CUT.

MOTION: springy bouncy friendly motion, slow orbit around the pot on shot 3;

AUDIO: wet stirring swirl, buzzing wings, a rising three-note chime under the

Characters only emote and gesture, they do NOT talk. No on-screen text.

## Bloco 3 — 20 a 30s

```
{ESTILO}
{PALETA}

SHOT 1 — 0.0s to 2.5s — EXTREME CLOSE-UP: the paint finishes turning a vivid
lime green and settles flat and glossy; a single bubble pops on the surface.
HARD CUT.
SHOT 2 — 2.5s to 5.0s — WIDE: Zuzu leaps into the air and Bibi loops around
him; petals puff up; behind them the grass of the clearing is exactly the same
green as the paint.
HARD CUT.
SHOT 3 — 5.0s to 7.5s — MEDIUM: Bibi lifts the stick and paints one big green
stripe across a smooth river stone, then turns to camera and gives a little
bow.
HARD CUT.
SHOT 4 — 7.5s to 10.0s — CLOSE-UP pulling back to MEDIUM: a new pot full of
cherry-red paint rolls into frame and stops beside the green one; Zuzu's ears
shoot up into the same question-mark shape as the opening block.

MOTION: springy bouncy friendly motion, celebratory pops, camera pulls back on
the last shot; movement from the very first frame.
AUDIO: soft bubble pop, rising marimba chord and tiny clapping on the reveal,
then the opening sparkle ding as the red pot arrives — no voice, no narration,
no music.
Characters only emote and gesture, they do NOT talk. No on-screen text.
```

**Legenda:** `VERDE!` na revelação, `E AGORA?` nos últimos 2s.
